# Provenance of the water-life icons

Drawn for the seas, rivers and marshes mockup (`docs/mockups/ocean.html`, M25)
by `tools/art/ocean_art.py` with the balatro-pixel-art kit (Sweaft-64
palette, hard alpha), 24 x 24, and copied here byte for byte when the water
life entered the game (`ocean-environments` 7.1). Mockup art until the
owner judges the creatures (Q2).
