# Mission icons

The mission tree's icons (`starter-missions` TS.4) are the game's own item
icons wherever M33 names one (`items/{tools,goods,stations,lights,fish,water_life}`).
Two M33 icons have no item:

| File | From | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `map.png` | `docs/mockups/mission-tree/items/icon-map.png` (11 px) | centred in a 16 px tile, unchanged |
| `yard-hen.png` | `docs/mockups/mission-tree/items/yard_hen.png` (36 px) | trimmed to its pixels and reduced to 16 px, nearest |
| `lost-villager.png` | drawn for `town-missions-tree` design 10 | 16 px by hand in code (a townsperson's head and shoulders and a question mark), a 1 px dark outline as the item icons have; the lost villager job's well on the board |
