# fire

The wildfire's own textures (`openspec/changes/wildfire` 4.2). Made in this
repository by a committed script; nothing taken from outside it.

| File | Size | What | Made by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `smoke.png` | 128 x 32 | four 32 x 32 cells, one smoke puff's life from tight to fraying; a five-tone grey ramp lit from the upper left, alpha its cover faded over the outer three texels; no outline; sampled nearest | `python3 tools/art/fire_art.py` |

The flames are the held torch's own sheet (`textures/held_light/flame.png`),
and a charred tree wears its species' own bark, darkened in the game.
