Puff cull and rain detail

Two changes, measured together against the commit before them:

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Run 2026-10-29 11:42Build a23842b + uncommitted changes GPU Apple M4 Max (Metal)Window 1440 × 900, uncapped

Findings

  • The flights are 2.5 ms faster at p50: clouds 19.74 -> 17.22 ms, storm 19.70 -> 17.12 ms, p95 4-5 ms lower.
  • About 2.1 ms of it is the wet cut. From the air nearly every wet pixel is past 20 m, and each one used to compute the rippled sheet and the ring layers and then fade them to nothing by its footprint. The GPU time up to the composite falls by the same 2.0 ms (15.0 -> 13.0).
  • About 0.45 ms is the puff cull (19.74 before against 19.27 cull-only, across two sittings, so read it as roughly half a millisecond). That fits what the puffs cost at all: about 1.9 ms in 2026-10-28-cloud-puffs. The cull keeps 250-400 of the 450-900 puffs over the horizon, from 2,080 built; the build costs the CPU 0.15-0.18 ms.
  • On the rain stroll the cut does not show at p50 (19.27 -> 19.15, inside the 0.3 ms spread): in a lane much of the wet ground is inside 20 m, and the stroll's frame is dominated by other work.
  • Neither change is visible where it should not be. The same cloudy frame before and after (docs/screenshots/puff-frustum-cull/) matches across the sky; 0.6% of pixels differ by more than 8/255, all in far wet grass, which is the wet cut. The street stills (docs/screenshots/wet-near-detail/before-after.jpg) show rings ending by 20 m and the ground past it wet, puddled and mirroring.

How this was measured

  • One machine, one sitting.
  • The stills are two --capture frames 900 steps in; the rain's own animation differs between them, so the difference image (difference-x6.jpg) also lights the near rings. The far grass past 20 m shows the real change: it loses the faint grass rings.
  • "GPU clouds" on this Mac spans the GPU work up to the composite, not only the clouds (see 2026-10-29-lighting-full).
  • The CSVs and logs are local (output/perf/2026-10-29-cull-wet).

Each run starts from a fresh world with the clock pinned at 10:00 and throws away its first 10 s. A cloud run that never found cloud, or a route that never landed, is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:

python tools/perf_suite.py --scenarios clouds,storm,rain-stroll --repeats 2 --out output/perf/2026-10-29-cull-wet --variant "before|output/perf/before-exe/pbd-app-a23842b||a23842b" --variant after
