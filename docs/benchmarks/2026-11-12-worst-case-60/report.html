Worst Case 60

The owner's goal for lighting-perf: "get worst case to 60", p99 within 16.7 ms in every scenario on the desktop. The final sitting: f15a647 (the lighting model as merged to main, its exe and its own assets, at its own default frame latency of 1) against the branch at c05ae5f (lighting-perf 2.1, 2.2, 2.4, 2.5, 2.8, 2.9, 2.10, frame latency 2, the loading screen, the towns' shared materials). RTX 3070 (Vulkan), i7-9700F, 1440 x 900, uncapped (--no-vsync), release builds, 2 repeats interleaved (A B B A), clock 10:00, then rain-stroll again at 22:00. Nothing else ran (the machine's shared lock held for the sitting).

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Findings

  • The goal is met: every scenario is inside 16.7 ms at p99. Each cell is the suite's median of the two runs (the higher of the two):
  • On foot in the rain (the last scenarios over the floor before 2.8) the frame is about half of f15a647's: the lane 13.9 to 7.2 ms p50 and 25.4 to 12.9 ms p99, at night 27.1 to 13.1 ms p99. Of that, 2.8 (L8's traces at half size) is the last 4 ms of p99 (docs/benchmarks/2026-11-12-reflect-half/), 2.9 (the rain at half size) and 2.1 the rest of the GPU, frame latency 2 the CPU-GPU overlap.
  • The town walk reads 6.11 / 11.32 and 7.32 / 14.22 ms (p50 / p99) in its two runs, against 16.3 / 23.5-23.9 for f15a647. Its two runs differ in how much wet lane they see (the same two modes as in the 2.8 sitting); the worse is still 2.5 ms inside the floor.
  • In flight p99 halves (clouds 22.7 to 9.6, storm 22.7 to 9.9 ms); most of that is frame latency 2, with GPU total down about 1.1 ms.
  • What is still over a budget is p95 against 120 fps (8.3 ms) on foot in the rain and in the town (10.7-11.3 ms), not the 60 fps floor. The frames over 30 ms that remain are the recurring storm-descent spikes (the other agent's loading-screen work) and single landings.
  • f15a647 runs at latency 1, its own default; that is main as the owner plays it, so the comparison is main against the branch, not one change. The per-change A/Bs are in the design.

How this was measured

  • Two repeats; the walk's and the town's runs differ by more than the other scenarios' (route and weather), so their cells carry about 1-2 ms of spread at p99.
  • Uncapped, windowed, real time; with vsync on the game shows the same frames paced to the display.

Each run starts from a fresh world with the clock pinned at 10:00 and throws away its first 10 s. A cloud run that never found cloud, or a route that never landed, is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:

python tools/perf_suite.py --scenarios clouds,storm,far-side,walk,rain-stroll,town --repeats 2 --out output/perf/2026-11-12-worst-case-60 --variant "f15a647|C:/Users/santi/repos/pbd-perf-main/output/perf/baseline-exe/lighting-f15a647/pbd-app.exe||f15a647" --variant "branch|C:/Users/santi/repos/pbd-perf-main/output/perf/baseline-exe/lighting-perf-2.8/pbd-app.exe"
