Town frame time

Two rounds on the town walk out of Ashenstead, each an interleaved A/B in one sitting on the owner's machine (RTX 3070, Vulkan, 1440 x 900, no vsync, 10:00, rain forced).

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Run 2026-10-08 18:39Build b3c5428 + uncommitted changes GPU NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 (Vulkan)Window 1440 × 900, uncapped

Findings

  • Round 2 (this page's tables): 82 to 98 fps mean, p50 10.5 to 9.1 ms. before is round 1's build, after adds decisions 5-7. Resting craft sleep: the craft stepped a tick fell from about 20 to under 2, about 2.9 ms a tick saved. A town's templates are no longer cloned every frame, and a town material is re-prepared about once a second, not every 45 ms. p99.9 fell from 131 to 45 ms.
  • Round 1: main 63 fps at p50 to 94-98 fps. Each town's lighting is no longer recomputed on every terrain landing (240-450 ms hitches), and the field corners stop recomputing a ground height that never changes (6-10 ms a frame). Its tables are in report.md's round 1 section, checked in beside this page.
  • From main to now, at p50: 16.0 ms to about 9.1 ms. GPU time is about 6 ms throughout, so the frame is close to the GPU now.
  • Still open: frames slower than 14 ms are 7% of the walk and are not periodic: they are the spread of an uncapped frame. A fresh world freezes for about 18 s at its start, in both builds, and is next to measure.

How this was measured

  • The owner was using the machine during round 2, so its spread is wider. Its two variants were interleaved, so both saw the same disturbance.
  • Only the town walk. The flights, storm and open-country walk are not measured in either round.
  • revision: b3c5428 (uncommitted changes)
  • adapter: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 (Vulkan); window 1440 x 900, uncapped (--no-vsync)
  • clock pinned at 10.0 h; first 10.0 s of each run thrown away; 2 run(s) per variant, interleaved
  • budgets: p95 within 8.3 ms (120 fps), p99 within 16.7 ms (60 fps); over marks a miss. Each cell is the median over the valid runs.

Each run starts from a fresh world with the clock pinned at 10:00 and throws away its first 10 s. A cloud run that never found cloud, or a route that never landed, is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:

python tools/perf_suite.py --scenarios town --repeats 2 --out output/perf/2026-10-08-cpu-round2 --variant "before|output/perf/exe-after/pbd-app.exe" --variant "after|output/perf/exe-cpu2/pbd-app.exe"
