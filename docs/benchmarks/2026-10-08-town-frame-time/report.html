Town frame time

The town-frame-time change against main, in one sitting, interleaved (main, after, after, main) on the owner's machine. The sitting was stopped after the town runs because the owner needed the computer. clouds, storm and walk were not measured.

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Run 2026-10-08 16:27Build 7ba872e GPU NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 (Vulkan)Window 1440 × 900, uncapped

Findings

  • Walking through a town: 63 fps to 94-98 fps at the median. Frame p50 is 16.0 ms on main and 10.2-10.7 ms after. p95 is 18.4 against 16.1-16.5 ms, and p99 21.1 against 17.7-18.4 ms (frames from 30 s on). The 60 fps floor holds at p95 after and does not hold on main.
  • The hitches near towns are gone. On main every fine-set landing re-baked every standing town's light, 240-450 ms over two frames (the trial walks earlier in the day: 34 frames over 150 ms in 101 s). In this sitting main's valid run had 8 frames over 50 ms after 30 s, and the branch had 1 and 2.
  • GPU time did not move (6.3-6.7 ms p50 in both builds). The win is all main-thread work: the generator's ground height, asked 4,616 times a frame by the field corners and up to 360,000 times a landing by the bake.
  • The frame is still CPU-bound. About 10 ms of wall time against 6.3 ms of GPU. The next cost has not been found yet. The weather suspects in the design (two cube uploads and 8,192 atmosphere samples a second, and the held-then-burst stepping) are the next instruments.
  • A fresh world freezes for about 18 s at its start, in both builds: frames of 8.7 s, 2.5 s and 6.8 s, 8-18 s after launch, while the world's sites are surveyed and its towns laid and saved. The last of the three falls after the suite's 10 s warm-up, so it is each row's max. The town change does not touch it. It is not yet known whether opening a saved world pays any of it.

How this was measured

  • One valid main run. The other stopped at 230 m (no WALK_DONE) when the sitting was interrupted, so main's row is that one run. The branch's two runs differ by 0.5 ms at p50, which is less than the difference measured.
  • Only the town scenario. Whether the flights, storm and open-country walk moved is not measured. The change only skips work that reads to the same values, so they are not expected to be worse.
  • RTX 3070, Vulkan, 1440 x 900, --no-vsync, clock 10:00, rain forced to 1, first 10 s thrown away.
  • revision: 7ba872e
  • adapter: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 (Vulkan); window 1440 x 900, uncapped (--no-vsync)
  • clock pinned at 10.0 h; first 10.0 s of each run thrown away; 2 run(s) per variant, interleaved
  • budgets: p95 within 8.3 ms (120 fps), p99 within 16.7 ms (60 fps); over marks a miss. Each cell is the median over the valid runs.

Each run starts from a fresh world with the clock pinned at 10:00 and throws away its first 10 s. A cloud run that never found cloud, or a route that never landed, is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:

python tools/perf_suite.py --scenarios town,clouds,storm,walk --repeats 2 --variant "main|output/perf/exe-main/pbd-app.exe" --variant "after|output/perf/exe-after/pbd-app.exe"
