Fit and cover capped

distance-lod-fade, "A runaway fit" and "A second loop": each fine set's fit and cover capped at a level's own margin, and the landing's dissolve at 1 s. Against 117aba8 (the distance cross-fade as pushed), interleaved.

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Run 2026-09-26 08:30Build 117aba8 + uncommitted changes GPU NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU (Vulkan)Window 1440 × 900, uncapped

Findings

  1. Fine sets build in a third of the time in flight. On cloud-hop the median build fell from 2.8-2.9 s to 1.3-1.4 s (off the runs' own logs), and a flight lands 25-26 sets instead of 13-14. The finest level keeps up with the camera and is under it when it lands (the owner's "nearest LOD doesnt seem to be loading in when landed").
  2. So cloud-hop draws more, and costs more. Its median frame rose from 6.21 to 6.87 ms and the GPU's from 5.15 to 5.84 ms, past the spread: with the detail no longer lagging hundreds of metres behind, the fine levels are drawn under the camera where the coarse ones stood in. That is the picture being right, not a new cost; it still holds 60 fps at p99.
  3. The far side and the walk are unchanged (4.33/4.39 and 4.54/4.54 ms).

How this was measured

  • One machine: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU (Vulkan), i9-11900H, mains power; 1440 x 900, uncapped. Two runs per variant.
  • revision: 117aba8 (uncommitted changes)
  • adapter: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU (Vulkan); window 1440 x 900, uncapped (--no-vsync)
  • clock pinned at 10.0 h; first 10.0 s of each run thrown away; 2 run(s) per variant, interleaved
  • budgets: p95 within 8.3 ms (120 fps), p99 within 16.7 ms (60 fps); over marks a miss. Each cell is the median over the valid runs.

Each run starts from a fresh world with the clock pinned at 10:00 and throws away its first 10 s. A cloud run that never found cloud, or a route that never landed, is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:

python tools/perf_suite.py --scenarios cloud-hop,far-side,walk --repeats 2 --out output/perf/2026-09-26-fit-and-cover --variant "before|output/perf/before-117aba8/pbd-app.exe" --variant "after|target/release/pbd-app.exe"
