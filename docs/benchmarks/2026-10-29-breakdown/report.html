Where the frame goes

The owner, of the flights' 19 ms: "Then what is the major cost". Apple M4 Max (Metal), release build at 6f85efe (the puff cull and the 20 m wet cut in), window 1440 x 900, uncapped, clock at 10:00, first 10 s thrown away, 2 runs of each variant interleaved, GPU apps closed. All 30 runs were valid.

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Findings

  • What each part saves at p50, switched off (clouds flight / rain stroll / 1 km walk; full frame 17.16 / 19.25 / 12.83 ms): rain passes (PBD_PASS_OFF=rain,overlay,lens,shower) 0.10 / 6.54 / 1.03 ms; the new lighting (PBD_LIGHTING=today) 2.16 / 5.41 / 1.69 ms; reflections, L8 (PBD_REFLECT=0) 1.42 / 3.85 / 0.70 ms; clouds (PBD_NO_CLOUDS=1) 1.98 / 0.35 / 0.00 ms. Shares overlap and do not add up to the frame.
  • On the ground in rain, the screen rain passes are the biggest single cost: 6.5 ms of the stroll's 19.3 (rain, overlay, lens and shower together). Which of the four is not split yet.
  • Next is the new lighting, 5.4 ms on the stroll, and 3.9 ms of it is the reflections: a lane in rain is wet ground near the eye, and each wet pixel walks the columns and the screen for its mirror. In flight it is 2.2 ms (reflections 1.4).
  • The clouds cost 2.0 ms in flight and almost nothing on the ground.
  • In flight about 13 ms is none of these: with the clouds or the new lighting off the flight is still 15 ms, and the rain passes barely show there at p50 (their p95 drops 5.6 ms). That floor is the terrain, the sky and atmosphere, the water, the near fog, the tone pass and the CPU side, and it is not broken down yet.

How this was measured

  • One machine, one sitting; shares overlap and are not additive.
  • "GPU clouds" on this Mac spans the GPU work up to the composite and is not shown here.
  • The CSVs and logs are local (output/perf/2026-10-29-breakdown).

Each run starts from a fresh world with the clock pinned at 10:00 and throws away its first 10 s. A cloud run that never found cloud, or a route that never landed, is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:

python tools/perf_suite.py --scenarios clouds,rain-stroll,walk --repeats 2 --out output/perf/2026-10-29-breakdown --variant full --variant "no-clouds||PBD_NO_CLOUDS=1" --variant "no-rain-passes||PBD_PASS_OFF=rain,overlay,lens,shower" --variant "today-lighting||PBD_LIGHTING=today" --variant "no-reflect||PBD_REFLECT=0"
