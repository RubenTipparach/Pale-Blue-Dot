Findings

  1. No cost the spread can see. The scenic clouds 6.67 ms at the median before against 6.43 after, cloud-hop 6.28 against 6.39; the clouds' GPU time on the cloud legs 3.66 against 3.66 and 3.82 against 3.85 ms.
  2. The picture moved where it was meant to. The limb frame (--route far-side --time 10 --frames 3000): the clip alone keeps the stair-stepped rim on limb clouds, the rebased edges remove it. The cloud-ghosting turn check: 0.000% of pixels changed turning, 0.003% still.

How this was measured

  • One machine: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU (Vulkan), i9-11900H, mains power; 1440 x 900, uncapped. Two runs per variant; clouds and cloud-hop only (the far side and the walk were measured against 964ddf4, unchanged here).
  • revision: 994078a
  • adapter: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU (Vulkan); window 1440 x 900, uncapped (--no-vsync)
  • clock pinned at 10.0 h; first 10.0 s of each run thrown away; 2 run(s) per variant, interleaved
  • budgets: p95 within 8.3 ms (120 fps), p99 within 16.7 ms (60 fps); over marks a miss. Each cell is the median over the valid runs.

Each run starts from a fresh world with the clock pinned at 10:00 and throws away its first 10 s. A cloud run that never found cloud, or a route that never landed, is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:

python tools/perf_suite.py --scenarios clouds,cloud-hop --repeats 2 --out output/perf/2026-09-25-edges-on-clip --variant "before|output/perf/before-bef7d58/pbd-app.exe" --variant "after|target/release/pbd-app.exe"
