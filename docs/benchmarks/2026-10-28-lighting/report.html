lighting model, step 1

## Findings

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Run 2026-10-28 21:41Build db25fe2 + uncommitted changes GPU Apple M4 Max (Metal)Window 1440 × 900, uncapped

Findings

  • The new shading costs about 0.6 ms. Cloud flight p50: today 10.5 ms, new without bloom or the tone pass 11.1 ms. That covers the sun after the air and the sky's nine coefficients per fragment, in the terrain and in the field-lit meshes.
  • Bloom costs about 2.4 ms, and its size does not change that. Bevy's Bloom measured the same at max mip 512 (14.2 ms), 256 (14.1) and 128 (13.9), against 11.8 without it, in one sitting. So the cost is not the blur. A cheaper bloom of our own is task 3.3.
  • The tone pass and the CPU meter cost nothing measurable. New without bloom (11.2) matches new without the tone pass (11.1).
  • The first build cost +6.6 ms, and that is fixed. The sky's light was projected per call, and in flight the sun's elevation at the camera changes every frame. Its single-entry cache missed on two threads. It is now a 513-elevation table built once and interpolated: new went from 17.1 to 14.1 ms.
  • Walk (first sitting, before the table fix): p50 9.2 to 11.3 ms. p95 and p99 were lower with new (14.2 and 20.7 against 17.3 and 27.7), inside the spread of two runs.

How this was measured

  • Apple M4 Max (Metal), window 1440 x 900, --no-vsync, release build, 2 runs per variant, interleaved. Only the cloud passes have GPU timers on a Mac, so a pass's own GPU time is inferred from variants.
  • Variants come from one build: PBD_LIGHTING=today draws today's model, PBD_NO_TONE=1 drops bloom and the tone pass, and PBD_NO_BLOOM=1 drops bloom.
  • Sittings: output/perf/2026-10-28-lighting-l126 (first build), -split (this page's tables), -split2 and -bloom (bloom).

Each run starts from a fresh world with the clock pinned at 10:00 and throws away its first 10 s. A cloud run that never found cloud, or a route that never landed, is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:

python tools/perf_suite.py --scenarios clouds --repeats 2 --out output/perf/2026-10-28-lighting-split --variant "today|target/release/pbd-app|PBD_LIGHTING=today" --variant "new|target/release/pbd-app" --variant "new-no-tone|target/release/pbd-app|PBD_NO_TONE=1"
