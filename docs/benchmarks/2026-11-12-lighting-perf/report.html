Lighting perf checkpoint

lighting-perf task 3.1, as a checkpoint toward the owner's "get worst case to 60" (p99 within 16.7 ms in every scenario). Three builds, interleaved, 2 repeats, on the owner's desktop: RTX 3070 (Vulkan), i7-9700F, Windows 10, 1440 x 900, release builds, clock at 10:00 unless named.

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Findings

  • Every scenario is faster at every percentile it can be compared at, p50 / p95 / p99 ms, f15a647 then final: - clouds: 9.89 / 15.85 / 24.05 to 6.27 / 10.55 / 13.62 - storm: 9.93 / 16.20 / 24.74 to 6.17 / 10.77 / 13.74 - far-side: 6.57 / 11.95 / 15.94 to 4.08 / 6.98 / 9.04 - rain-stroll: 14.03 / 18.30 / 27.93 to 9.98 / 15.63 / 20.37 - rain-stroll at 22:00: 14.13 / 21.65 / 29.42 to 9.84 / 16.20 / 21.12 - town (rain): 17.33 / 24.87 / 29.99 to 9.90 / 16.54 / 22.01
  • The 60 fps floor at p99 is met in flight (clouds, storm, far-side: 13.6, 13.7, 9.0 ms) and not yet on foot in the rain (rain-stroll 20.4 by day, 21.1 at night; town 22.0). With vsync on, 3.9% of the stroll's frames, 1.0% of the town's and 0.5% of the storm's are over 16.7 ms.
  • Most of the gain is frame latency 2: 3.2-4.3 ms at p50 in every scenario on its own (2026-11-12-frame-latency). The fixes add 0.5 ms p50 and 2.7 ms p99 on the stroll (2.1), 0.26 ms p50 in the storm (2.2), and a third off the puffs' build (2.4); the loading screen took the storm's p99 from 18.0 to 14.9 ms (2026-11-12-fixes).
  • On foot in the rain the frame is the GPU's: GPU total p50 is 9.5 ms against a wall p50 of 10.0 ms. The GPU by pass on the stroll: terrain (with L8) 4.43 ms, the sun's cascades 2.08, the rain volume 1.50, the opaque pass 0.51, the lens 0.29. The tail is GPU-bound frames the queue paces unevenly (tools/tail_classes.py).
  • The lighting model still costs 2.8 ms there: final against PBD_LIGHTING=today in the same exe, 9.95 against 7.18 ms p50 (GPU 9.42 against 6.60). The spec's 1.0 ms is not met yet.
  • The rain volume's cuts (2.10) saved nothing measurable: 1.50 ms of GPU against 1.44 before. They are exact and stay in; the pass's cost is elsewhere (the shells' streaks and haze), which is the half-resolution task.
  • Spikes over 30 ms are mostly not the game's repeatable work. In the storm, 4 a run recur at the same route metres in every run of every build (3544, 3808, 4122 m and touchdown, on the descent; one is a fine-set landing with an 11.6 ms upload). The rest, and nearly all of the walk's, fall at different places in each repeat. Closing the Solitaire game (12:20) changed nothing: 14-30 a run closed, 23 open, same exe, p99 20.2-20.7 against 20.3.
  • The town's own work: final's town walk recurs at 33 of 47 spikes in its two runs (the town bake), which the town agent's batching takes.

How this was measured

  • walk and town cannot be compared across the loading screen. With it, the walk's world opens in a full storm (cover 1.00, rain 1.00; before, 0.69 and 0.00), so final's walk is a walk in the rain (9.89 / 15.48 / 20.69 against a dry 9.47 / 11.86 / 14.88). This is a loading-screen bug being fixed in its own worktree. The town forces rain in every build and is comparable.
  • cloud-hop is invalid in every build: its route found no cloud at 10:00 on this seed.
  • Warm-up: f15a647 and before start their warm-up at the first frame and then freeze about 6 s for the towns' ground; final starts at READY. The scripted routes are the same, but the stretch measured differs by a few seconds.
  • The machine: Chrome, Steam, Discord and Spotify were open in every sitting (6-11% of the machine; Spotify used 49-56% of one core from about 13:00). The Solitaire game was open until 12:20.
  • Two repeats; the quiet walk three. A difference under the spread is not a result.
  • The raw CSVs and logs are local: output/perf/2026-11-12-lighting-perf* and output/perf/2026-11-12-walk-quiet.
  • revision: cdf0317
  • adapter: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 (Vulkan); window 1440 x 900, uncapped (--no-vsync)
  • clock pinned at 10.0 h; first 10.0 s after loading (the ready line, else the first frame) thrown away; 2 run(s) per variant, interleaved
  • budgets: p95 within 8.3 ms (120 fps), p99 within 16.7 ms (60 fps); over marks a miss. Each cell is the median over the valid runs.

Each run starts from a fresh world with the clock pinned at 10:00 and throws away its first 10 s. A cloud run that never found cloud, or a route that never landed, is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:
