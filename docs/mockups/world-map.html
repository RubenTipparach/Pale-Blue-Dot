What is real here

The planet, its biomes at each size, the temperature, the sea ice and the fish ranges are drawn from rasters the game's own functions made (pbd_core::map::base_texel, the atmosphere run 200 days, and the fish rules in tools/fish_ranges.py). The clouds and rain are the simulated atmosphere sampled every two game hours through one day.

The settlements are placeholders: the city-sites rules run here, in the page, on 14.7 m pixels, and their names come from made-up syllables. Step 2b places them for real.
