Pale Blue Dot Sounds

The planned sounds of the planet, before any of them is in the game: 191 files for 117 cues and beds. Footsteps and digs for every ground the player can stand on, swimming, fishing, the craft, rain, thunder near and far, fire, the menus, and the ambience beds that tell a meadow at noon from a jungle at night, a beach, a town, a cave or the sea from under it. 183 are cut from real recordings (CC0 only: Kenney's packs and Freesound sounds whose pages say Creative Commons 0), slowed, layered and filtered to fit; 8 are synthesised where nothing recorded serves (menu tones, the stall warning). Placed sounds are mono and will be heard from where they happen; beds are stereo and play under everything, quiet and far.

A mockup: nothing here plays in the game yet (openspec/changes/audio-and-ambience, task 2.3). Listen and keep or send back each take; a cue with several takes (step_grass_1 to _6) is played one take at a time in the game, never the same twice running. Each file is written at its own peak level (about -1 dBFS for placed sounds, -9 to -14 for menu tones, -14 for beds); the balance between cues in play is set by the game's data, not by this page. Claude cannot listen; these were chosen by measurement and a spectrogram sheet, and only your ears approve them.
