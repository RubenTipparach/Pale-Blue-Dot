About the tracker

Every Pale Blue Dot page in one place: the roadmap, the game mockups with their approvals, the guides, and everything else (benchmark reports, gate pages, surveys).

A mockup is approved for build first, then for the game. Nothing goes into the game without both.

  • Listed: not built yet.
  • Drafted: published, waiting on you.
  • Approved for build, then Approved for game: tick them on a mockup's card. The game approval opens after the build one.

Roadmap

What the game is built toward, in order. Each step links the mockups that show it and its OpenSpec change. A step's known issues (what is not done yet) are in the survey, under its phase; the link on the step opens them.

Game mockups

Each mockup with its stage, its two approvals and your notes.

Guides and Misc

Guides are reference pages to read and point at. Misc is everything else published for Pale Blue Dot, newest first.

The index

The index on the left jumps to any section, phase, step or mockup; every entry is a link you can share (for example #roadmap-phase-3-npcs). On a phone it opens from the Index button.
