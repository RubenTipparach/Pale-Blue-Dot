Pens, Nests and Pedigrees

All Pale Blue Dot mockups (the tracker)

The animal kingdom of every world in one tree, and keeping animals on Tenebris: taming wild ones, pens and coops, what each needs and gives, eggs that hatch, young that grow, and breeding that passes traits from both parents. The owner: "we need a whole animal kingdom and domestication of animals, eggs, breeding". None of it is in the game yet; today the game's only animals are eight fish.

About this mockup, and which art is whose

It extends the animals mockup (M7, animals.html: its species, biomes, taming by a trust meter and drops), the seas and rivers mockup (M25, ocean.html: its 41 water creatures and ducks with eggs), the fish lab (M9: a creature rolled from a species and a seed, rare morphs 1 in 64), the planets mockup (M1: Sequoia's, Wick's and Fallow's species) and farming (M8: pens beside the fields, compost from muck). It contradicts none of them, and redraws none of their art. Dishes made from meat, eggs, milk and honey are in the cooking mockup, drafted beside this one; this page lists them only as products.

The game's own art, copied byte-identical into husbandry/game/: the hex ground is the shipped terrain atlas (assets/tilesets/atlas.png, the fields sheet read the way planet_surface.wgsl reads it); fences, coop, barn, stable, sty, hives and the farmhouse are settlement textures (assets/textures/settlement/) at their repeat_m; the eight fish in the Kingdom are the game's item icons (assets/items/fish); the HUD is the game's debug line and hotbar slots. Other mockups' art, copied byte-identical: M7's portraits, hides and drops (husbandry/m7/), M25's creatures (m25/), M9's fish (m9/), M1's species and Fallow's fleece (m1/), M8's wheat, turnip, kale and compost (m8/). Mockup-only, new here: the domestic breeds and new species (yard hen, cockerel, chick, rootling, fellrunner, herd hound, hedge cat, yard dabbler, clover bee, field mouse), their hides, the products, tools and needs icons, and the straw and mud floors, all pixel art drawn in code with the balatro-pixel-art kit (tools/art/husbandry_art.py, which reuses M7's body-plan functions): Sweaft-64 colours, hard alpha, shown only at whole-number scales. Water, glass and the sky are flat colour, as the game draws them. The 3D animals are built from boxes, as M7 builds them, each box wearing its hide.

Rendered for review in a cloud container with no GPU (Chromium on SwiftShader): the screenshots say how it looks, never how fast it runs.

What you are looking at

A farmstead on Tenebris's fields, on hex cells 2.833 m across and 1 m a layer. Around the farmhouse: a coop and its run (yard hens and a cockerel pecking), a barn and pasture (plodders grazing), a sheep pasture (woolbacks and two ridge goats, worked by a herd hound), a sty (a rootling sow with her piglets in a line behind her), a pond with yard dabblers and their duck house, a stable and paddock with two fellrunners, hives beside the wheat, troughs in every pen, a kennel and a compost bin. A hedge cat keeps the barn.

At night

Hens go in and the coop shuts; grazers lie down together; ducks sleep on the water. The lanterns at each gate, the coop and barn doors, the stable and the farmhouse windows each light a tight pool; the moon is dim and the ground between is dark. After dark a red fox (M7) comes for the coop. A shut coop is safe, and the hound runs it off. Turn the hound off or leave the coop open to see what the owner is asked in Q2.

taming | fields | 10:00

Feeding a trust meter (M7's rule, with numbers)

Crouch with the animal's favourite food in hand and it lets you to arm's reach (1.5 m). Each feed fills its trust by its species' gain times its temperament factor (1.3 for a calm one, 0.6 for a wild one, from its genome). Stand or run inside its fear radius (9 m, M7's) and it bolts and loses 10. Full trust: it follows you for a minute, or while it wears a lead; through a pen's gate it is yours, and its record is written at once on the durable path.

The other four ways

Raise young: boarlets from a sounder become rootlings; pups from a pale wolf den, visited while the pack hunts at dusk, become herd hounds. Hatch: an egg from a wild nest hatches tame, which is how ducks are kept (a wild dabbler is protected, M25; its egg is not). Swarm: a spring swarm on a branch moves into an empty hive at dusk with a smoker. Moves in: a barn with feed draws field mice, and a dish of milk draws a hedge cat, which is never penned. Wild only: predators, migrants, hares, swarms of light and every fish (tanks are M9's). Try the red fox in the list.

Eggs

A hen lays in a nest box once a day. With a cockerel in the coop her egg is fertile: an item that carries its seed and both parents' ids, as M9 keeps a fish's seed in its slot. A fertile egg hatches under a broody hen (a hen with the broody trait sits on up to 8 and stops laying) or in an incubator (6 eggs, needs its lamp lit with oil or tallow). Hen's eggs take 3 world days, duck eggs 4; an egg left cold for a day will not hatch and is an egg to eat.

Young

Stages come from the birth day and the world day, so nothing about growth is saved. A chick is a pullet at 5 days and a laying hen at 10; a world day is 48 minutes of play (M8), so that is eight hours. Mammals carry their young: a woolback 5 days for one or two lambs, a plodder 8 for a calf, a rootling 4 for a litter of 4 to 8, a fellrunner 10 for a foal. Young follow their mother.

The animal kingdom

Every animal on every world, from the game, M7, M25, M9, M1 and this page, in one tree grouped by body form. Life arose separately on each world, so a Sequoian crane is bird-form, not kin to Tenebris's birds; three alien branches fit no Tenebris class. Click a branch to filter, or filter by world, biome, class and role.

Breeding

Pick a sire and a dam from the herd. The screen shows every offspring the pair can give, before you pair them: each gene's four equally likely values (ticks) and the range they span, the coat, pattern and rare-morph odds, and the cost of a close pairing. Roll a few births to see it hold; click anyone for their pedigree.

Click once for the sire (blue), again on a female for the dam (rose).

Selective breeding, ten generations

The same inheritance run forward: a woolback herd bred for wool, keeping the best ram and ewes of each generation. A closed herd breeds only its own young; rotating rams buys in one unrelated ram each generation. Both raise the yield; the closed herd pays for it in inbreeding, which lowers hardiness.

The husbandry economy

What a herd eats and gives in a world day, by season, and how many of M8's crop cells it takes to feed. Change the counts. Products are scaled by each animal's yield gene; these are a herd of middling yield (1.0), all content. Meat, eggs, milk and honey are ingredients for the cooking mockup, which has the dishes.

A year is 100 days (orbit-and-seasons). Grass grows in every season but winter; in winter grazers eat hay.

What the journal says

Every line names who wrote it. The player taming, naming, moving and butchering is @p. The farm's own changes are the husbandry world process, run by the steward each in-game hour, and it yields: a proposal that would change a field the player wrote, or a piece the player placed, is refused whole.

Saved games survive

Wild animals are derived from the world seed, the herd's seed and the clock, and never saved; retuning them never touches a save. A kept animal is a fact: its genome is written when it is tamed (rolled from its wild seed) and never re-derived, so a later change to how genomes roll cannot change it. Its species is saved as its body and roster index, which is append-only. The record has its own schema version in identity.ron: a later gene arrives with a default on open, and the old version stays readable. Ended animals keep their records, so no pedigree breaks. Husbandry changes no terrain, so no generator version moves.

Towns

A town's livestock is the town's: records written by its process (@wtown:<settlement>). Taking one is refused, as M25 says. Buying one from the farmer (M5) moves its owner to the player in one @p line.

Decisions / questions for the owner

Three questions have something to look at; they go in the survey with their screenshots. The rest are recommendations taken (ask only with screenshots), recorded in the change so they are never mistaken for the owner's words.

  1. Q1. The Kingdom by body form, with three alien branches, or by world? Look at the Kingdom tab. Recommendation: by form, with world as a filter, so a drover and a woolback read as kept the same way. Screenshot docs/screenshots/mockup-husbandry/kingdom.jpg.
  2. Q2. Can a fox take a hen? At night on the Farmstead, turn the hound off and open the coop. Recommendation: a fox can take one hen from an open, unlit run, never from a shut coop or past a hound; the hen's record ends "taken by a fox". A loss the player can always prevent. Screenshots farm-night.jpg, farm-night-fox.jpg.
  3. Q3. The breeding screen: ticks on a range, or arrows? Look at the Breeding tab. Recommendation: every possible value as ticks on its range, as drawn, with a better/same/worse arrow beside each as a summary. Screenshot breeding.jpg.
