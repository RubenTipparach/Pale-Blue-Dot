The mission tree

The game's Missions screen (the pack's MISSIONS tab, opened with O), redrawn as the tree that starter-missions designs ("Missions are a tree that unlocks the tech" and "The full tree"). Every mission, objective and unlock on this page comes from there.

What the tree is for

The owner, 2026-11-15: "The point of the missions is to get the player to play through the content and unlock new tech", and "a comprehensive mission tree that would encourage the bot to try all these game play mechanics". Every mechanic the game has today has a mission that needs it: hands, tools at three tiers, building, light, water, fishing, farming, herding, mining and smelting, cooking and eating, and getting about.

  • The trunk, hands to tools, runs left to right and opens everything else.
  • Fishing, farming, herding, mining and smelting, light and building open when the trunk is done, and can be played in any order.
  • Getting about opens after "Go fishing".
  • Cooking opens after fishing's "Three kinds of fish" and one of farming ("Harvest") or herding ("Collect from the herd"): the dashed lines. The dotted lines mark where a cooking mission uses a crop or an egg from another branch; they do not lock it.

How unlocks work

  • A recipe or station is locked until the mission that names it is done. In the recipe list (the Recipes button here) it shows as a dim silhouette with the name of that mission, and the game says nothing else.
  • The unlock list is data on each mission (unlocks: [recipe codes]) in missions.ron, so tech and missions are tuned in one file. A recipe no mission names is unlocked from the start.
  • On a card, an unlock is a silhouette until its mission is done, then it lights.

Old saves keep everything

  • An unlock is derived from the world's missions record and never stored on its own.
  • A world from before the tree opens with every mission it already finished done.
  • Any recipe it has already crafted, and anything the player carries or placed, counts as unlocked too, so no old world loses a tool it could make. A test pins this.
  • Mission ids stay append-only; the old line's ids map onto the tree's.

New in the tree NEW

  • Objective kinds: fell, eat, pour, night, reach, swim, board. Each is raised where the game already acts: felling, eating, the bucket, the clock, the walker's position against a site or the sea places map, the swimmer, and boarding.
  • Recipes: fence, gate, campfire and torch. Today they come in the starting kit, which keeps them for a world from before the tree.
  • Both are marked NEW on the cards and in the side panel.

The bot plays it as the gameplay demo

The phase-1 showcase is a bot whose goal is this tree. It plays the trunk, then each branch in order: fishing, farming, herding, mining and smelting, cooking, light and building, getting about. Each branch is a goal and a section of the video. It plays every objective through the player's inputs and never sets a count or an item; waits on a crop, an egg or the night are sped up and captioned. A branch it cannot finish fails the run and the log names the stalled objective: that is the test that every mechanic is reachable by play.

The Bot run controls walk that order here. Reset is a new world; Step takes one step (a mission becomes Now, an objective fills, the mission is Done and its unlocks light); Play runs the steps at the chosen speed. The page opens on a world partway through, the bot in the middle of "Go fishing". Nothing moves until Step or Play is pressed.

Reading the page

Drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, Fit to see the whole tree. Click a mission for its full objectives, help line and unlocks. Far out, a card shows only its icon and title.

Art

Panels, plaques, slots, checks and bars are the game's own stone-and-carved-wood kit (assets/ui/stone, assets/ui/oak); item icons are the game's (assets/items), kept with their 1 px UI outline. The yard hen is the husbandry mockup's sprite and the map glyph the UI mockup's, since the game has no item icon for either. Where a mission has no icon of its own (a rowboat, a town), it borrows the nearest good. Help lines for missions that exist today are the game's (missions.ron); the others are drafted for this mockup.
