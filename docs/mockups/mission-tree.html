The mission tree

Six tabs: a tree each on Starter (#starter), Town (#town) and Power (#power), as the game's Missions screen will have them; Survival (#survival), the starter tree as a Survival world shows it; Crew (#crew), the crew missions of multiplayer; and Mills (#mills), the power tree's windmill and water mill in 3D.

The POWER tab (version 10) NEW

The owner, 2026-11-26: "draft up a reasearch tree mockup (addon to current research tree mockups) for power generation: windmills - for processing food; water mills for generating hydro power - this can power some appliances once they are built; power lines to carry electricity, no battery storage for awhile". The tab draws early-power design 2 and 3, phase 4's infrastructure part. Written up, not built.

  • A tree of its own, like the town tree: its own file and saved record. It opens when the starter tree's "The iron age" is done, because the first mission needs an iron axle; opening it unlocks the wooden gear and the axle.
  • The trunk, Millwright: cut gears and an axle, dress millstones. Then four branches.
  • Wind: raise a windmill on a rise, grind grain to flour, bake a loaf, grind feed mash for the herd, and set the town tree's grinder on the mill floor to crush stone by the wind. The windmill is mechanical: it grinds, it makes no electricity. It needs wind at the mill: the sails stand below 3 m/s, run faster up to 8 m/s, and are furled above 20 m/s. A rise is M20's turbine rule: the cell stands 2 layers above every cell within 3.
  • Water: draw copper wire and wind a dynamo, set a water mill on a flowing river, make 6 kWh. A mill makes up to 6 kW, by the river's flow (full at 1.2 m/s); a frozen river or a shut sluice makes none.
  • Lines: wood poles at most 4 cells apart (M20's poles), copper wire, lamps on the line, and the lesson: When the wheel stops, shut the sluice at night and the lamps go out at once. There is no battery in this tier: power is used as it is made.
  • Appliances (proposals): a motor on the grinder (2 kW, twice as fast), bellows on the forge (1.5 kW), a motor on the mixer (2.5 kW). With more on the line than the wheel makes, everything on it slows by the same share and the lamps dim.
  • Recipes: the RECIPES side tab gives each unlock's station and inputs, all from goods the game has (planks, logs, rope, fibre, stone, iron and copper ingots, wheat, turnip, leather).
  • It leads into M20: M20's tier II wind turbines (150 kW) and hydro dam (500 kW) come later with heavier wire on the same poles; nothing here changes their numbers.
  • The tab opens with the bot partway through "String a line". The cross-tree needs (the grinder and the mixer from the town tree) are hidden gates, named in the help lines.
  • Icons for the new goods are stand-ins from tools/art/early_power_icons.py.

The MILLS tab (version 10) NEW

A windmill on its rise and a water mill on the river, joined by a pole line, as early-power design 12 draws them. One 3D view, made when the tab first opens. Built of the game's settlement textures (plank, timber, fieldstone, thatch, iron, poles, grass, dirt, stone), sampled nearest; the sails, the wheel's boards, the river and the wire are new 32 px textures from tools/art/early_power_art.py. At night the line's lamps, the mill's windows and an oil lantern at the windmill's door light what is near them; the moon stays dim. Nothing turns until Turn is pressed. Shut sluice lifts the wheel out of the race: the line's lamps go dark at once, since nothing stores power; the windmill's lantern, not on the line, stays lit. Drag to turn, wheel or pinch to zoom, arrow keys and + and - from the keyboard.

The SURVIVAL tab, version 10: fishing and farming under Survival NEW

The owner, 2026-11-26: "can you move farming and fishing under the survival tree too?", "it seems more logical to make it a follow up after making a fire", "so the player has all basic tools first". In a Survival world the Fishing and Farming branches are drawn under the Survival branch as 8 · SURVIVAL · FISHING and 8 · SURVIVAL · FARMING, each a follow-up to Make a fire. "Make a fire" comes after "Stone tools", so the axe, pickaxe and shovel are all made by then: no extra gate is needed. The bot plays them right after "The first night". The Starter tab (Explorer worlds) is unchanged.

The SURVIVAL tab (version 8) NEW

The owner, 2026-11-26: "start working on phase 4, we're gonna work on survival mode which will add new branch to starter missions to handle survival". The tab draws survival-mode design 18: the starter tree with one more branch, 8 · SURVIVAL, shown only in Survival worlds. Written up, not built.

  • One more branch, not a third tree: the survival loop is the starter loop with its pressures on. Every mission in it is marked mode: survival and never shown or counted in an Explorer world.
  • It hangs off "Open the pack", the trunk's second mission in the game (missions.ron; this tab adds it to the trunk drawn here), so the first thing a Survival player is told is to drink: water empties first.
  • Its trunk: drink water, something to eat, a spear, a hunt, cook meat (waits on Make a fire), the first night (unlocks the bedroll, which sets where you wake).
  • Hunting and weapons off the hunt: dress a kill, tan a hide, bow and arrows, a long shot, a hunting axe (waits on Smelt bronze), a sword, drive off a predator.
  • Heat and cold off drinking: a cactus straw, cactus juice, out of the heat, warm by the fire (waits on Make a fire), dress for the weather.
  • The body in water off eating: a long swim, hold your breath, into the water.
  • Fire (wildfire) off cooking: relight a torch the rain put out (waits on Light the night), put out a fire.
  • Rewards are unlocks, as on the starter tree: the branch opens the survival recipes in the order a player needs them.
  • The tab opens with the bot partway through "Hunt": the trunk, drinking, eating and the spear done.
  • Icons on this tab for things the game does not have yet (spears, bow, arrows, hunting axe, sword, straw, hide, the marks for heat, cold, breath and the moon) are stand-ins from tools/mockup_survival_icons.py.

The CREW tab (version 7) NEW

The crew missions of coop-missions (design 7: "The panel shows the crew and each share"). A crew is whoever is in the world; its progress is the world's, counted by the server, so a player who joins halfway sees it as it stands and adds to it. Each objective shows the crew's total and a short bar per player in their colour, so nobody carries the crew unseen.

  • Raise a house together, H1 to H7: clear the site, gather timber, lay the floor, raise the walls, set the ridge beam, roof it, light the hearth. When the hearth is lit the house belongs to everyone who built it.
  • The crew rules (design 3 and 4), shown only when they hold a mission back: "Needs another pair of hands" (an objective two or more must share), "Waiting on Cy" (everyone must add a share), and the ridge beam, a step for two: two players at its two ends within 3 s, "Needs two of you at once". In single player the step for two counts as met, so the chain can still be finished alone.
  • Drafted: Log drive, Bucket chain, Fish and fry and The town's order, each waiting for its systems to move behind the server.

The Crew run controls play three players, Ana, Bo and Cy, through the chain. The tab opens partway through, with the walls five of six up and Cy's wall still to come. Nothing moves until Step or Play.

Two choices for the owner, with this tab's screenshots in the survey: the site marker (a map marker shared by the crew, recommended, or the first floor piece) and the first chain's reward (the house itself, recommended, or coins or a tool).

The town tree (version 5, phase 3)

The owner, 2026-11-22: "can you add a new mission tree for phase 3? this would be an entirely separate tree since phase 3 is mostly interacting with the town". The Town tab draws town-missions-tree's design 2: every mission, objective, unlock and reward on it comes from there. Written up, not built.

  • Its own tree: its own file, its own saved record (town_missions), its own tab. No mission of one tree is a parent of a mission of the other.
  • It opens the first time you reach a town, whatever the starter tree's state: trade, the board and the people need no tools, and a player who walks to a town early should be shown what to do there. Missions that need tools ask for things the starter tree teaches (planks, rope, iron) as craft objectives.
  • The trunk, Town: meet the townsfolk, buy and sell, a job from the board. Then five branches: Trade (trade in earnest, carry a parcel, goodwill), Jobs (three jobs, a lost villager, mend a building, light the lamps), Roads (grinder, crushed stone, mixer, gravel, mark a road with survey stakes, pave it, join a town), Carts off "Pave it" (wheels, a hand cart, haul a load, buy a horse, hitch up, 500 m on the road), Houses and logs, and Carpentry (version 6, furniture design 12: a carpenter's bench, a chair, take a seat, a table, a bed, lie in a bed, a chest, furnish a room, look in a town chest; it unlocks the iron-banded chest and the closet).
  • Every mission pays coins, shown with the coin on each card: 5 to 60, 640 in all (525 before Carpentry's 115), paid in the same saved line as the mission's done mark, so never twice. A horse costs 120 at a stable.
  • Unlocks are the new recipes and stations: grinder, crushed stone, mixer, gravel, survey stakes, wheel, hand cart, horse cart (townsfolk design 41, land-transport design 10), locked until their mission by the starter tree's own rule.
  • NEW marks an objective kind the game does not raise yet: talk, job, goodwill, stake, mark, pave, haul, hitch, build, float.
  • Light the lamps reads NOT YET: the game raises no lamp-lit event, so it is pending, as the starter tree's mechanism has it, and the bot leaves it.
  • Old saves: no record they hold changes. A world that has been to a town (a purse, goodwill, a taken job, a lost villager, or "Find a town" done) opens with the tree at its root, nothing paid for work done before.
  • The Town tab opens with the bot partway through "Mark a road": both stakes driven, four cells of eight marked. The coin count beside the step number is what the tree has paid so far.
  • Icons: the grinder, mixer, crushed stone, survey stakes, wheel, carts, horse, board, heart, house, speech bubble and townsperson are stand-ins drawn for this page (tools/mockup_town_tree_icons.py) in the item set's manner; the game's own come when the things are built. Coin, gravel, parcel, planks, log and the lantern post are the game's.

The starter tree

The game's Missions screen (the pack's MISSIONS tab, opened with O), redrawn as the tree that starter-missions designs ("Missions are a tree that unlocks the tech" and "The full tree"). Every mission, objective and unlock on this page comes from there.

What the tree is for

The owner, 2026-11-15: "The point of the missions is to get the player to play through the content and unlock new tech", and "a comprehensive mission tree that would encourage the bot to try all these game play mechanics". Every mechanic the game has today has a mission that needs it: hands, tools at three tiers, building, light, water, fishing, farming, herding, mining and smelting, cooking and eating, and getting about.

  • The trunk, hands to tools, runs left to right and opens everything else.
  • Fishing, farming, herding, mining and smelting, light and building open when the trunk is done, and can be played in any order.
  • Getting about opens after "Go fishing".
  • Cooking opens after fishing's "Three kinds of fish" and one of farming ("Harvest") or herding ("Collect from the herd"): the dashed lines. The dotted lines mark where a cooking mission uses a crop or an egg from another branch; they do not lock it.

How unlocks work

  • A recipe or station is locked until the mission that names it is done. In the recipe list (the Recipes button here) it shows as a dim silhouette with the name of that mission, and the game says nothing else.
  • The unlock list is data on each mission (unlocks: [recipe codes]) in missions.ron, so tech and missions are tuned in one file. A recipe no mission names is unlocked from the start.
  • On a card, an unlock is a silhouette until its mission is done, then it lights.

Old saves keep everything

  • An unlock is derived from the world's missions record and never stored on its own.
  • A world from before the tree opens with every mission it already finished done.
  • Any recipe it has already crafted, and anything the player carries or placed, counts as unlocked too, so no old world loses a tool it could make. A test pins this.
  • Mission ids stay append-only; the old line's ids map onto the tree's.

New in the tree NEW

  • Objective kinds: fell, eat, pour, night, reach, swim, board. Each is raised where the game already acts: felling, eating, the bucket, the clock, the walker's position against a site or the sea places map, the swimmer, and boarding.
  • Recipes: fence, gate, campfire and torch. Today they come in the starting kit, which keeps them for a world from before the tree.
  • Both are marked NEW on the cards and in the side panel.

The bot plays it as the gameplay demo

The phase-1 showcase is a bot whose goal is this tree. It plays the trunk, then each branch in order: fishing, farming, herding, mining and smelting, cooking, light and building, getting about. Each branch is a goal and a section of the video. It plays every objective through the player's inputs and never sets a count or an item; waits on a crop, an egg or the night are sped up and captioned. A branch it cannot finish fails the run and the log names the stalled objective: that is the test that every mechanic is reachable by play.

The Bot run controls walk that order here. Reset is a new world; Step takes one step (a mission becomes Now, an objective fills, the mission is Done and its unlocks light); Play runs the steps at the chosen speed. The page opens on a world partway through, the bot in the middle of "Go fishing". Nothing moves until Step or Play is pressed.

Reading the page

Drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, Fit to see the whole tree. Click a mission for its full objectives, help line and unlocks. Zoom steps through set levels, so the card frames stay crisp: far out a card is its icon and title, then its frame alone; close in, the cards and their type grow.

Art

Panels, plaques, slots, checks and bars are the game's own stone-and-carved-wood kit (assets/ui/stone, assets/ui/oak); item icons are the game's (assets/items), kept with their 1 px UI outline. The yard hen is the husbandry mockup's sprite and the map glyph the UI mockup's, since the game has no item icon for either. Where a mission has no icon of its own (a rowboat, a town), it borrows the nearest good. Help lines for missions that exist today are the game's (missions.ron); the others are drafted for this mockup.
