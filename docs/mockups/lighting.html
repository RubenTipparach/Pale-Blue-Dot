L1Sun colour from the air Transmittance along the sun's path: same as today at noon, gold at 10°, red at the horizon. GPU: 0 ms
L2Sky light probe + ground bounce Fill from the real sky as 9 SH coefficients, and the sunlit ground lighting things from below. GPU: ~0 ms (9 MADs)

L5Reflection probe A cube of the world around the eye, for water, the wet ground and metal. Today these reflect a sky with nothing in it. GPU: ~0.1–0.2 ms

Rain and wet ground

Knobs marked both are the game's own (weather.ron, rain_*) and move both sides. Knobs marked new only exist in the new wet model. Today's wet ground mirrors a dim fixed sky colour, and its glint asks only where the sun is, not where you are looking from, so a wet field reads as darker, not shinier. The new one is a glossy film: it reflects what is really around it (L8, screen-space reflections, falling back to the sky), and catches highlights from the sun, the moon and every lamp.
