A mockup of the four held tools, drawn directly on the hex grid, not converted from the 16 px icons. Each handle is one straight row of hexes, and each head is built from the grid's own straight lines, at 0°, 60° and 120°. Nothing here is in the game yet.

How this would go into the game

  • The hex model becomes the source. Each tool is stored as a list of hexes, one colour each, in a RON file. The mesh is built from it, as pbd_core::hexel builds it now: hexagon fronts and backs, a side only where the neighbouring hex is empty, and faces shaded by direction.
  • The slot icons are drawn from the same hexes, as a small front view, so the icon and the model cannot disagree. This reverses what happens now: today the model is sampled from the icon, and that sampling is what made the handles zigzag.
  • Grip and head are fixed on the grid: the grip is at (0, 0), and the head is at the far end of row 0. The held pose places those two points exactly as held.ron does today.
  • The rod has no line or float: the game draws the real fishing line from the rod tip.
