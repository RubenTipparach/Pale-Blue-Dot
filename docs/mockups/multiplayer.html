Multiplayer: host, join, another player

The two mockups of openspec/changes/multiplayer tasks 5.1 and 5.2, for the owner's approval before any game UI or body is built. Version 1, 2026-11-27. Three tabs, each a bare link: Host (#host), Join (#join) and Player (#player). Only the Player tab draws 3D, and its view is made when the tab first opens.

Host (task 5.1, design 14.3)

  • The pause menu as the game draws it today (desktop/menu.rs: RESUME, SAVES, SETTINGS, MAIN MENU, QUIT under the PAUSED lintel) gains HOST, second. Today hosting is --host <port> on the command line (design 20); this is the screen that replaces it.
  • Before hosting: a port (47123, the one the two-machine test used), an optional password, and SHOW ON LAN, which makes the game appear in other players' On LAN list. A port outside 1024 to 65535 is refused on the panel; Taken in the bar plays a port already in use.
  • While hosting: the address others join by (this machine's LAN address and the port) with COPY, and who is connected, up to 8 (design 14.2). Each guest has KICK and BAN; BAN asks first. STOP asks first when someone is on. The world keeps going under the menu; RESUME goes back to play while still hosting.
  • Joins and leaves show as a line over the hotbar's place, the way the game's toasts read. Use A player joins and A player leaves in the bar.
  • No relay and no public address yet (design 20: a direct address or the LAN among friends); the address shown is the LAN one.

Join (task 5.1, design 9)

  • The title menu (menu.rs: CONTINUE, NEW WORLD, LOAD WORLD, SETTINGS, QUIT) gains JOIN. Joining leaves no world of yours open, so it is on the title, not the pause menu.
  • The Join panel: your NAME (sent in the Hello), an ADDRESS with its port, and ON LAN, the games hosts on this network show. A game with a password shows a lock and asks for it; a full one shows FULL and cannot be pressed.
  • The host answers in the bar picks what the host says to the next JOIN, so every message can be seen. The messages are short and plain, one line each:
The protocol's answerThe player reads
JoinRefusal::GameVersionGame version differs
JoinRefusal::WorldVersionWorld version differs
JoinRefusal::DifferentWorldDifferent world
JoinRefusal::FullGame is full
JoinRefusal::BannedBanned from this game
JoinRefusal::WrongPasswordWrong password (the password field again)
No answer (the connect times out; not a refusal)No game at that address
  • "Game version differs" and "Different world" are design 9's words. With the launcher (game-launcher design 8) the first may grow a second line telling the player to restart it; that waits for the launcher.
  • A Welcome goes to the loading screen while the world's regions arrive (design 9: it stays up until they and every pipeline are ready).

Player (task 5.2, design 14.5)

  • A patch of the fields on hex columns (2.833 m across, 1 m layers) with the game's terrain tiles, a packed path, the game's trees and lamp posts. You stand at the near end; Wren walks the path. Close, Near, Middle, Far and the slider send Wren to that distance; Wren walks there and stops, then turns to face you. Nothing moves until you ask.
  • The name over them only when near: the name is drawn in the kit's font over the head, at the UI's whole-number scale, within 24 m, and not past it (recommendation taken, ask only with screenshots: about eight cells, where a face can still be told apart). It shows and hides in a fifth of a second as the line is crossed, and does not linger.
  • The same figure as the game's townsfolk (version 2, owner, 2026-11-27: "humans should be consistent with the NPCs in the game"): another player is M5's townsperson, the figure the game draws for its NPCs, built by the same kit (multiplayer/figure-kit.js, copied from npcs.html) and rolled from the Fields folk's palette. In the game another player is drawn by the townsfolk's own code (multiplayer design 24), so the two cannot drift apart.
  • Lit like the ground: the terrain's sun with its shadows, and at night the lamps' light per pixel with the towns mockup's falloff (1 - (d/R)^2)^2, every lamp lighting what is near it wherever the camera is. The moon is held at 0.035, so outside the pools the ground and Wren read near black: in the game that is LitByField and LitLikeTerrain on the body's root.
  • Eyes is your own view (drag to look, arrow keys too). Orbit Wren turns about the body to see it close (drag, wheel or pinch to zoom).

Which art is whose

  • The game's: the UI kit's oak frame, plaques, tabs and stone lintel, slot well, check, bar and tooltip (assets/ui/, the smooth renders, sampled linear as the game samples the kit); the kit font (assets/fonts/kit-font.json) and VT323 for body lines; the terrain atlas (assets/tilesets/atlas.png); the timber and iron of the lamp posts (assets/textures/settlement/). The backdrops are game captures (docs/mockups/ui/game/). Provenance: docs/mockups/multiplayer/README.md.
  • Mockup-only: the figure's tiles are the townsfolk mockup's (tools/art/npcs_art.py); the title backdrop is a still of the UI mockup's own demo.
