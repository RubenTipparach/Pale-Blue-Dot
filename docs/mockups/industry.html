Pits, plants and power

All Pale Blue Dot mockups (the tracker)

Industry on the hex grid: an open pit cut a layer at a time, the machines that turn its ore into plates, a siphon that cleans lake water and, much later, a mast that draws matter out of the ether, and the power ladder that runs all of it, from a coal plant to fusion, antimatter and a Dyson swarm round the star. Drag to turn, wheel to zoom. Night is on the toggle; at night only lamps, furnaces and vents light the ground.

About the art

What is the game's, what is new

The game's own, copied byte for byte (docs/mockups/industry/game/README.md): every hex column's top and side is a tile of the terrain atlas (assets/tilesets/atlas.png): the fields sheet's grass, dirt, limestone and copper ore in limestone; the mountains sheet's slate, granite and iron ore; the redwood sheet's ancient charcoal as the coal seam; the basalt wastes' ash, basalt and sulfur round the geothermal plant; the beach sheet at the lake. Walls, yards and the spoil heap are the settlement textures (assets/textures/settlement, at the manifest's repeat_m); the trees and lamp posts are built as the game builds them; the pickaxe, shovel, hand, torch, lantern and brazier are the game's item icons. Mockup-only, pixel art drawn in code with the balatro-pixel-art kit (Sweaft-64): the machine, generator and item icons, and the machine paint, belt, concrete, hazard stripe, white cladding, furnace grate and ether textures (tools/art/industry_art.py); the raw ore, coal and ingot icons are the crafting mockup's (tools/art/crafting_art.py); the habitat metal and glass are the building mockup's; the home world's map in the space view is the planets mockup's, and the star's pixel surface is drawn in the page from Sweaft-64 tones. Water is a flat tint, as the game draws it. Numbers are first guesses, chosen to be easy to change.
