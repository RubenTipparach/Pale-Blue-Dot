Pits, plants and power

All Pale Blue Dot mockups (the tracker)

Industry on the hex grid: an open pit cut a layer at a time, the machines that turn its ore into plates, a siphon that cleans lake water and, much later, a mast that draws matter out of the ether, the power ladder that runs all of it, from a coal plant to fusion, antimatter and a Dyson swarm round the star, and oil: a field under the ground, a pumpjack over it, a drilling rig, an offshore platform that is a rig and a power plant in one, a distillation column and an oil plant on the ladder. Five tiers dig the pit (hired townsfolk, an excavator, a quarry frame, a bucket-wheel, a drone hive), and section 6 moves it all: wires, pipes, belts, pneumatic tubes and freighters. Drag to turn, wheel to zoom. Night is on the toggle; at night only lamps, furnaces, flares and windows light the ground. Machines stand still until Run machines is pressed.

About the art

What is the game's, what is new

The game's own, copied byte for byte (docs/mockups/industry/game/README.md): every hex column's top and side is a tile of the terrain atlas (assets/tilesets/atlas.png): the fields sheet's grass, dirt, limestone and copper ore in limestone; the mountains sheet's slate, granite and iron ore; the redwood sheet's ancient charcoal as the coal seam; the basalt wastes' ash, basalt and sulfur round the geothermal plant; the beach sheet at the lake. Walls, yards and the spoil heap are the settlement textures (assets/textures/settlement, at the manifest's repeat_m); the trees and lamp posts are built as the game builds them; the pickaxe, shovel, hand, torch, lantern and brazier are the game's item icons. Mockup-only, pixel art drawn in code with the balatro-pixel-art kit (Sweaft-64): the machine, generator and item icons, and the machine paint, belt, concrete, hazard stripe, white cladding, furnace grate and ether textures (tools/art/industry_art.py); the oil chain's pumpjack, column, oil plant, crude, fuel oil, diesel and asphalt icons and the asphalt, flare-stack band and lit-window textures are drawn the same way in the same script; the oil field and the map under it are noise from a seed, computed in the page, as the game would derive them; the raw ore, coal and ingot icons, and the polymer standing for polymer feed, are the crafting mockup's (tools/art/crafting_art.py); the habitat metal and glass are the building mockup's; the home world's map in the space view is the planets mockup's, and the star's pixel surface is drawn in the page from Sweaft-64 tones. Water is a flat tint, as the game draws it. Numbers are first guesses, chosen to be easy to change.
