About this mockup: the idea, and which art is the game's

Every fish in Pale Blue Dot is drawn from a species and a seed. The species fixes the body plan, the fins, the palette and the size range. The seed rolls one individual: its length, its build, where its spots and bars fall, and now and then a rare colour morph. The same fish always comes out the same, so a trophy in a tank is the fish you caught.

Nothing here is in the game yet. Which art is whose: the eight fish the game already has use the game's own 16 × 16 icons (assets/items/fish), and the aquarium room is built from the game's settlement textures (plank, stone, plaster, timber, iron, sand; assets/textures/settlement), all copied byte for byte into fish/game/. Mockup only: the five new species' icons, drawn in the game icons' manner, the pattern swatches and the decor icons are pixel art drawn in code with the balatro-pixel-art kit, the solitaire collection's style (tools/art/fish_art.py, Sweaft-64 colours, shown at whole multiples). The 3D fish are this page's generator: low-poly, faceted bodies with 64 × 32 pixel-art skins sampled nearest. A person drew none of the new art; it is placeholder art for the owner to judge.

1 · The generator

Fish lab

Pick a species and roll individuals. Every roll stays inside the species: 85 to 125% of its length (the rule in fishing-and-equipment, section 7), a small spread in depth, width and fins, and a pattern placed by its own seed. Move a slider to step outside the species and see what each number does. Drag the fish to turn it.

Weight is Fulton's condition factor, W = K · L³, with K set by the body plan (eel 0.15, fusiform 1.0, globe 2.6) and scaled by depth and width. The ruler under the fish is in centimetres; the amber band on it is the species' range.

2 · Variation

Species sheet

Six individuals of every species, seeds 0 to 5 of one school. Each row is drawn at one scale, so the size spread is real: the largest fish in a row can be half again the length of the smallest. Amber marks a rare colour morph (1 in 64). Click a fish to open it in the lab.

These thumbnails are baked: PNGs in fish/sheet/, made by tools/mockups/bake_fish_sheet.js from this page's own renderer, so the sheet opens no 3D view. The lab still renders live.

3 · Thirteen species

Roster

The eight from fishing-and-equipment keep their water, temperature window, strength and hook window as written there. Five are new here, to cover a lake, the deep sea, the under-ice ocean of an ice moon and the volcanic vents. Rarity, bait, time of day and season are new proposals. A season is where the temperature window puts the fish through the year, because the spawner reads the water temperature now (climate-and-fish-maps).

4 · The parts list

Parts and patterns

A species is a choice from each of these lists plus its numbers. Six pattern types, seven body plans, six tails. The swatches are the pixel-art versions; in 3D each pattern is painted as pixel art onto a 64 × 32 skin from the fish's own seed, in Sweaft-64 colours, and drawn with nearest filtering so its pixels stay square.

5 · Keeping fish

Aquarium

A tank is built from glass cells on the hex grid: one cell is 2.833 m across the flats and 1 m tall, the Tenebris gold standard, so with the sand and the air gap under the lid it holds about 6,000 litres of water. The tank checks what lives in it: the water type, the temperature, room to swim, who eats whom, and what each species needs. The fish school with the same boids the sea uses. It opens at night: the tank's lamp lights the room, the lantern and the candle light their corners, and the moon in the window stays dim. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

6 · How it would be built

In the engine

PieceProposalWhy
SpeciesNew fields beside each species in assets/config/fauna.ron: body (plan, depth, width), fins, tail, pattern (type, density, contrast), palette (back, belly, accent, fin), morphs, rarity, bait, active (time of day).One record per species already holds its numbers and its field-guide text. The look joins them, so the guide, the icon and the fish cannot disagree.
An individualRolled from hash(school seed, index), the hash the design already uses for a fish's length. The same hash places its pattern and picks a morph.Deterministic: a caught fish keeps its look in a slot, in a tank and after a reload, with nothing extra saved but the seed.
DrawingOne mesh per body plan, bent in the vertex shader by a swim wave. The per-fish numbers (length, depth, width, fin scale, hue, pattern seed) go in the instance buffer, and the pattern is computed in the fragment shader from the seed, so a school is one instanced draw.The design draws a school in one instanced draw. Canvas skins per fish, as this page does, would not hold that at 34 fish a school.
A caught fishThe slot line gains the seed: f{species},{n} becomes f{species}:{seed},{n} for a fish kept whole. Older lines with no seed read as seed 0.Saved games stay playable: the old format keeps reading, and the roster stays append-only.
A tankGlass cells are blocks; the tank is the set of joined glass cells with water in them. Its contents are a stored record (species, seeds, decor) on the durable path, like any edit.A tank is something the player made. CLAUDE.md: nothing a player made is dropped.
