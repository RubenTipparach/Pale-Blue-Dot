## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sand dug in a desert is desert sand

Digging a generated sand layer in a cell whose biome is the desert SHALL give
one desert sand block, a material of its own. Digging generated sand anywhere
else, or any placed sand, SHALL give sand. Desert sand SHALL be drawn from the
desert sheet wherever it is placed, and its pack icon SHALL be that sheet's
top. Generation SHALL be unchanged by this: no new generator version, and a
saved world's sand SHALL stay sand.

#### Scenario: Dug in the dunes, put back in the dunes

- **WHEN** a player digs the desert's sand and places it back in the hole
- **THEN** the block is desert sand, drawn as the desert's top, and digging it
  again gives desert sand

#### Scenario: Dug on the beach

- **WHEN** a player digs a beach's sand
- **THEN** it gives sand, drawn from the beach sheet wherever it is placed

#### Scenario: An old save

- **WHEN** a save written before desert sand existed is opened
- **THEN** every placed sand block in it is still sand, and the ground is
  generated by the version the save names, cell for cell
