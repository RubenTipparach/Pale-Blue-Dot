# Star Systems Specification

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Each star system is its own frame
The game SHALL hold each star system as a separate system frame with `f64`
coordinates and orbital time, and SHALL make the system part of every body's
identity and every world id. Adding a system SHALL NOT change any existing
body's id, generator or save.

#### Scenario: The home save after a system is added
- **WHEN** the Nocturne system is added to a game whose save names Pale Blue
- **THEN** the save opens on the same world, cell for cell, with no migration of its terrain

### Requirement: Every body in every system sits on the ladder
Every body with cells, in any system, SHALL have a radius on `R = 300 m * 2^(L - 7)`
at its level `L`, so its cells are the same 2.833 m hex and 1 m layer as every
other body's.

#### Scenario: A ported Tenebris body
- **WHEN** Sequoia (360 m in Tenebris) is ported
- **THEN** its radius is a rung of the ladder (4,800 m at L11), not 360 m

### Requirement: Ships cross between stars only by star lanes
A ship SHALL NOT fly faster than its Newtonian limits to reach another star.
Travel between systems SHALL be by a star lane: a pair of lane gates, one in
each system, that carries a ship from one to the other with nothing simulated
in between, and rebases it into the destination system's frame at a tick
boundary.

#### Scenario: A passage to Nocturne
- **WHEN** a ship enters Aubade's star-lane gate aligned and slow
- **THEN** it leaves Nocturne's gate in Nocturne's frame at the speed it entered, after the lane's passage time

### Requirement: Ported worlds are new generators
A world ported from Tenebris SHALL be generated by its own generator id at
version 1 and SHALL NOT change the generator any existing world names.

#### Scenario: Pale Blue after Sequoia is ported
- **WHEN** Sequoia's generator is added
- **THEN** Pale Blue still generates with TENEBRIS v5, cell for cell
