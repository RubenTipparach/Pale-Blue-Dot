# Star Systems Specification

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Each star system is its own frame
The game SHALL hold each star system as a separate system frame with `f64`
coordinates and orbital time, and SHALL make the system part of every body's
identity and every world id. Adding a system or a body SHALL NOT change any
existing body's id, generator or save.

#### Scenario: The home save after a system is added
- **WHEN** the Tallow system is added to a game whose save is on the home world, Tenebris
- **THEN** the save opens on the same world, cell for cell, with no migration of its terrain

### Requirement: The home world is Tenebris, unchanged
The home world SHALL be named Tenebris and SHALL keep its generator
(`TENEBRIS` v5), its radius (4,800 m at L11) and its orbit. Its name SHALL be a
display label only, so renaming it SHALL NOT change any save or generator.

#### Scenario: A save made before the rename
- **WHEN** a world saved while the home world was shown as "Pale Blue" is opened
- **THEN** it opens on Tenebris with `TENEBRIS` v5, cell for cell, with no migration

### Requirement: Every body in every system sits on the ladder
Every body with cells, in any system, SHALL have a radius on `R = 300 m * 2^(L - 7)`
at its level `L`, so its cells are the same 2.833 m hex and 1 m layer as every
other body's.

#### Scenario: A body from Tenebris
- **WHEN** Sequoia (360 m in Tenebris) joins the home system
- **THEN** its radius is a rung of the ladder (4,800 m at L11), not 360 m

### Requirement: Ships cross between stars only by star lanes
A ship SHALL NOT fly faster than its Newtonian limits to reach another star.
Travel between systems SHALL be by a star lane: a pair of lane gates, one in
each system, that carries a ship from one to the other with nothing simulated
in between, and rebases it into the destination system's frame at a tick
boundary.

#### Scenario: A passage to Tallow
- **WHEN** a ship enters Aubade's star-lane gate aligned and slow
- **THEN** it leaves Tallow's gate in Tallow's frame at the speed it entered, after the lane's passage time

### Requirement: Bodies from Tenebris are new generators
A body brought from Tenebris into the game (Sequoia, Crag, Frostbite and their
moons) SHALL be generated by its own generator id at version 1 and SHALL NOT
change the generator any existing world names.

#### Scenario: Tenebris after Sequoia is added
- **WHEN** Sequoia's generator is added
- **THEN** Tenebris, the home world, still generates with `TENEBRIS` v5, cell for cell

### Requirement: Gas giants have no landing
A gas giant SHALL have no surface, no cells and no landing. A craft SHALL be
able to work in the giant only by floating: docked at a floating platform or
as an airship in the giant's airship band, a declared depth range below its
cloud tops.

#### Scenario: Flying down into Amber
- **WHEN** a ship descends into Amber's atmosphere
- **THEN** there is no ground to land on; it can dock at the floating skimmer platform or deploy an airship that floats in the airship band

### Requirement: A craft below a giant's limit depth is destroyed
Each gas giant SHALL declare a limit depth below its airship band. A craft of
any kind or tier that goes below that depth SHALL be destroyed. The giant's
sea of liquid hydrogen SHALL lie far below the limit, so no craft reaches it.

#### Scenario: An airship pushed past the limit
- **WHEN** an airship in Amber descends below 1,500 m under the cloud tops
- **THEN** it is destroyed, and the hydrogen sea, 7,200 m under the cloud tops, is never reached

### Requirement: No moon orbit intersects a ring
A ringed body's rings SHALL lie inside the orbit of every one of its moons:
each moon SHALL orbit outside the outer ring radius, in the world and wherever
the giant's system is drawn at its own scale.

#### Scenario: Amber's moons in the orbit view
- **WHEN** Amber, its rings and its moons Cinder, Rime and Murk are drawn
- **THEN** every moon's orbit, less the moon's drawn radius, lies outside the outer edge of Amber's rings
