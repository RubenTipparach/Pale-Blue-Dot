## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Logs in a river flow with it and stop where it stops

A log placed in flowing water SHALL be carried by the water's flow, and SHALL
come to rest where the water stops flowing or is too shallow to float it.

#### Scenario: A log dropped upstream

- **WHEN** a log is dropped into a river
- **THEN** it drifts downstream with the current and stops in the still water where the river ends

### Requirement: Logs can be carried on a raft

A raft SHALL carry logs in its hold, loaded and unloaded by the player.

#### Scenario: Loading a raft

- **WHEN** the player puts logs into a raft's hold and poles it downstream
- **THEN** the logs travel with the raft and can be unloaded at the other end
