## T. The mission tree and unlocks (design "Missions are a tree that unlocks the tech")

- [x] T.1 `unlocks` on missions and a recipe lock in `pbd_core` (one rule, used by the pack, the bench, the forge and the campfire). Verify: core tests that a locked recipe cannot be crafted, a mission's completion unlocks its list, and a recipe no mission names is open.
  - `MissionEntry::unlocks` (resolved and checked against the recipe book), `Chain::unlocked_by` and `Chain::recipe_open`. The app derives `missions::RecipeLocks` each frame from the chain, the record and the world, never stores it; `craft::press` (pack and bench), `forge::work` and `kitchen::work` refuse a locked recipe with "Opens with: <mission>". - [ ] T.2 The full tree in `missions.ron` (design "The full tree": trunk, fishing, farming, herding, mining and smelting, cooking, light and building, getting about), with the new kinds `fell`, `eat`, `pour`, `night`, `reach`, `swim`, `board` and the new recipes `fence`, `gate`, `campfire`, `torch`. Verify: the load test; every branch is reachable from the trunk; every `unlocks` names a real recipe.
  - First pass, built: the trunk (`gather`, `axe`, `wood`, `planks-rope`, `crafting-table`, `stone-tools`), fishing (`rod`, `go-fishing`), farming (`find-seeds`, `till`, `plant`, `harvest`), herding (`fence`, `tame`, `pen`, `collect`), mining and smelting (`dig-deep`, `ore`, `forge`, `smelt`, `bronze-tool`, `iron`), cooking (`fire`, `cook-fish`, `bread`, `eat`, `farm-meal`, `fried-egg`, `feast`) and light and building (`build`, `torch`): 31 missions with their `unlocks`.
