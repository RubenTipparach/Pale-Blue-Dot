## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The player can speed up and skip the clock
The pause panel SHALL offer clock speeds of 1×, 10×, 60× and 300×, and
buttons that move the clock one hour or one day ahead. Everything that waits
on the world clock SHALL move on with it.

#### Scenario: Sixty times as fast
- **WHEN** the speed is 60× and a frame of one second passes
- **THEN** the clock moves sixty seconds
