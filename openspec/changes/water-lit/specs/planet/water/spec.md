## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The water is lit by the light that reaches it
The water's body, its murk seen from below and the seabed's tint SHALL be an
albedo times the light that reaches the water: the sky's light by the cover
and the sun after the air, as the terrain is lit. No part of the water SHALL
be brighter than that light makes it, so the sea darkens with the land at
dusk, under cloud and at night. The water's reflection SHALL be the sky's own
radiance along the reflected ray where nothing nearer is mirrored, and its
haze SHALL be the terrain's.

#### Scenario: Night over the sea
- **WHEN** the sun is 29 degrees under the horizon and the sky is clouded over
- **THEN** the sea is darker than the beach beside it, both lit by the same night sky

#### Scenario: Deep and shallow
- **WHEN** the sea is seen from the shore at noon
- **THEN** the water over the shallows is lighter and greener than the deep water beyond them
