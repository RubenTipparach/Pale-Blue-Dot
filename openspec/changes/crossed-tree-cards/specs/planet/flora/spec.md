## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Far trees are fixed crossed cards
A tree past the near ring SHALL be drawn as fixed vertical cards crossed
through its trunk axis, standing on the local up at angles set by its own
turn, and SHALL NOT be drawn as a card turned to face the camera. The far
ring SHALL draw two cards square to each other; the mid ring SHALL draw
those two and the two between them, and the change between the rings SHALL
dither out only the cards the far ring does not draw. Every card of a tree
SHALL be lit with the one normal.

#### Scenario: Turning the camera over a forest
- **WHEN** the camera turns or moves sideways over far trees
- **THEN** no tree's cards turn with it

#### Scenario: From the ground at the far ring
- **WHEN** the player stands among far trees on flat ground
- **THEN** each far tree shows its two crossed cards and no cap

### Requirement: A tree seen from above shows its crown
A tree drawn by cards SHALL carry one fixed horizontal crown cap, showing
the tree baked from straight above, lit with the same normal as its cards,
and drawn only while the eye is above the cap.

#### Scenario: A forest seen from high above
- **WHEN** the camera looks down on a forest from a hundred metres or more up
- **THEN** each far tree reads as a crown from above
- **AND** no tree is drawn as a streak pointing at the middle of the view
