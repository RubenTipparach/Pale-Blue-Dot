# Animal meters: domestic and happiness

## Why

The owner, 2026-11-20, while the bot waited on a penned hen that laid
nothing: "would be nice to see a "domestic meeter" and a "hapines meeter"
for these guys", and "domestic means how much they like you, happiness is if
they are fed and in the right env". The hen was content (grazing and space)
but still recovering health from her unpenned days. Nothing on screen says
so.

## What changes

When the player looks at an animal (the F prompt's range), a small card
beside the prompt shows:
- **Domestic**: how much the animal likes the player. For a wild animal it
  is its trust, the same value the feed hearts show. For a kept animal it is
  a bond that grows with hand feeds and fed, cared-for days, and wears down
  when it is left hungry. It is shown as hearts out of five.
- **Happiness**: whether it is fed and in the right place. Its mood out of
  100, from the four needs (food, water, shelter, space), with an icon for
  each, lit when met. A thin health bar under it turns red when sick; a sick
  or unhappy animal makes nothing.

## Not in this change

- What the bond does beyond showing (better yields, following, breeding).
  It is written as a question for the owner in the design.
