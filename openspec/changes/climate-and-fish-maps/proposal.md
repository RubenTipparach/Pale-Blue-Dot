# Proposal: climate and fish maps (step 2c of the cities plan)

## Why

**The owner (2026-09-27): "I think we planned some fish stuff out before, we
need an in game map to show where fish spawn as well ... All of these I expect
a 2d map I can view ... c) climate map, fish map".**

The fish were planned, and the plan drew maps:
- `fishing-and-equipment` (design section 7) wrote each species' water and
  temperature rules.
- `examples/fish_ranges.rs` with `tools/fish_ranges.py` drew range maps for
  them off the real planet, into `docs/wiki/fish-ranges/`.

Those maps are outside the game. In the game, the only way to learn where a
species lives is to fish until it bites. The field guide (J) lists each
species' water and temperature window, but not where on the planet that is.

The same is true of the climate. The M key's weather overlays show the
temperature, the rain and the wind now. Nothing shows what a place is like
across the year: which coasts freeze in winter, where it is always dry. That
is the question a player choosing where to settle, or where to fish, is
asking.

## What Changes

- **A fish layer on the map.** For the species the player picks in the
  legend, it shows where a school can spawn now, by exactly the rule the
  spawner uses. It also shows the species' range through the year: water where
  it lives all year, and water where it comes and goes with the seasons, the
  way the field-guide range maps do.
- **The field guide links to the map.** Each species' entry gains its range
  thumbnail and a "show on map" action, which opens the map on the fish layer
  for that species.
- **A climate layer on the map**, from a year of the planet's own weather:
  - mean temperature;
  - the warmest and coldest seasons;
  - yearly rain;
  - sea ice (frozen all year, or frozen in winter);
  - a climate class from those.

  A season slider scrubs through the year.
- **The climate is baked, not simulated in the game.** A year of the
  atmosphere takes about 22 minutes to run (the `fish_ranges` log: 2,660 s for
  200 days, and a year is 100 days). An instrument runs it once and writes the
  normals as a committed asset. The asset records the seed, the generator
  version and the atmosphere settings it was made from. The game refuses to
  show the asset for a world it was not made from, and says so when the
  weather has been retuned since it was made.
- **The mockup first**, as part of the `world-map` mockup, using the real
  `fish_ranges` fields.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `world/climate`: the planet's climate normals. They are a year of the
  atmosphere, measured by an instrument, shipped as an asset with provenance,
  and checked against the world that reads them.

### Modified Capabilities
- `player/map` (added by `world-map`, not yet in the main specs): added
  requirements for the fish layer and the climate layer.
- `world/fauna`: no requirement changes. The fish layer reads the spawner's
  own rule (`Species::lives_in`, `fits`, `water_class`) and adds nothing to
  it.

## Impact

- **`pbd-core`:**
  - `climate` holds the normals' format, its provenance header and its
    lookup;
  - `examples/fish_ranges.rs` grows into (or is joined by) the climate
    instrument;
  - `fauna` gains a pure function for "can a school of this species spawn
    here now". The spawner calls it, so the spawner and the map share one rule.
- **`pbd-app`:**
  - two map layers: fish, and climate with its season slider;
  - the field guide's entry gains a thumbnail and "show on map".
- **Assets:** `assets/climate/tenebris-v<gen>.bin`, or PNG planes with a
  manifest, a few MB at 1,024 × 512. It is derived, so it is regenerated by the
  instrument rather than edited.
- **Docs:** `docs/wiki/fish-ranges/` is regenerated from the same instrument
  run, so the wiki and the game show the same ranges.
- **Performance:** the fish layer's "now" mask is rebuilt on the pool when the
  map opens and each in-game hour while it stays open, at 1,024 × 512. The
  climate layer is one texture lookup. Nothing runs while the map is closed.
