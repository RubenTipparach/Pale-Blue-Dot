## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A happy, fed hen lays an egg every six in-game hours, and feeding adds up
A kept hen SHALL lay one egg per 6 in-game hours while her happiness is at least 50 and she is not sick, and each hand feed SHALL add its food time to what she already has, up to the cap.

#### Scenario: Fed twice
- **WHEN** a content hen is hand fed twice on the same day and left a day
- **THEN** she has laid 4 eggs and has food for about 3 more days
