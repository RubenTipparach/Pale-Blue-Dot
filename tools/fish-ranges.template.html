    How the game would use it

    The rule a map shows is the rule the spawner would run. When a school spawns, the spawner reads three things at the candidate cell: its water class (river, shallows, shelf or deep, from the terrain), the water temperature now (Atmosphere::sample(dir).temperature), and whether the water is frozen. A species can spawn only where its class matches and the temperature is inside its window. The maps apply the same rule to the year's coldest and warmest daily means, so "part of the year" is where the rule passes in some seasons and not others.
